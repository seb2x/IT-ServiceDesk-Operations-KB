## 1. Purpose & Scope
This procedure governs the end-to-end lifecycle of IT hardware, software, and service acquisitions. It defines the rules for Requisitioning, Purchase Order (PO) creation, CapEx vs. OpEx expense classification, receiving inspection, and departmental charge-backs.

---

## 2. Financial Classifications: CapEx vs. OpEx

To maintain accurate financial tracking and asset accounting, all IT acquisitions must be classified prior to order submission:

* **Capital Expenditures (CapEx):** Physical assets purchased for long-term operational use value above the capitalization threshold ($1,000+ per unit).
  * *Examples:* Enterprise servers, core switches, firewalls, workstation fleets, laptops, network cabling infrastructure.
* **Operational Expenditures (OpEx):** Day-to-day operational expenses, recurring software subscriptions, consumable hardware, and ongoing maintenance contracts.
  * *Examples:* SaaS software licenses, cloud services, printer toner, replacement cables, annual vendor support contracts.
